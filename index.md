![Ops Essentials logo](assets/logo-144.png)

Operations features for **Jira Service Management Cloud** (Standard and Premium plans): know when a scheduled
job stops running, measure how your team responds to alerts, keep stakeholders informed and keep every alert
searchable.

- **Heartbeats.** One address for each backup, cron job or integration. If the job stops checking in, or
  reports a failure, an alert opens in Jira Service Management for the team you choose and follows its on-call
  schedule. The next successful ping closes it.
- **Reports.** Time to acknowledge and resolve (median, average and 90th percentile), share acknowledged
  within 5 and 15 minutes, after-hours load, noisiest alerts and workload per responder and team.
- **Stakeholder updates.** Short updates to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat or e-mail when alerts open,
  are acknowledged or close, with rules by priority, team and tag. Stakeholders need no Jira license.
- **Alert archive.** Every alert is copied into the app's storage on your site, kept as long as you choose,
  searchable and exportable to CSV.
- **No servers of ours.** The app runs on Atlassian Forge and keeps its data in your site's Forge storage.
  Data leaves Atlassian only towards the messaging channels you configure.
