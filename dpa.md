*Last updated: 29 October 2026.*

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the agreement under which the customer ("Customer") uses
the Atlassian Marketplace app **Ops Essentials for Jira Service Management** (the "App") provided under the
Steadyseam brand by **Sasso di Matera Giuseppe S.a.s.** (Via Clefi 2, 20146 Milano (MI), Italy, VAT
IT13043470965) ("Provider"). It applies to personal data processed by the App on behalf of the Customer, as
required by Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and by similar laws.

## 1. Subject matter, nature and purpose

The App monitors the Customer's scheduled jobs (heartbeats), copies the alerts of the Customer's Jira Service
Management site into an archive used for search and reports, and sends alert updates to the channels the
Customer configures. Processing consists of receiving, storing and transmitting that information, only to
provide the App.

## 2. Personal data and data subjects

- **Data subjects:** the Customer's users who own, acknowledge or close alerts; the administrator who set up
  the connection; the recipients of stakeholder e-mails; any person named in alert messages or heartbeat
  texts.
- **Personal data:** names or account identifiers of alert owners and responders; the Atlassian account ID of
  the administrator who set up the connection; e-mail addresses of stakeholder recipients and senders; any
  personal data the Customer's users or systems write in alert messages or heartbeat texts.
- No special categories of personal data are intended to be processed.

## 3. Duration

For as long as the App is installed, plus the retention periods in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md),
which the Customer controls through the App's settings. On uninstallation, Atlassian deletes the data stored
by the App according to its data retention policy for Forge apps.

## 4. Provider obligations

The Provider:
1. processes personal data only on the documented instructions of the Customer, given by installing,
   configuring and using the App;
2. ensures that anyone authorized to process the data is bound by confidentiality;
3. implements appropriate technical and organizational measures (Article 32 GDPR). The App runs only on
   Atlassian's Forge platform with no servers of the Provider, limits its settings to Jira administrators,
   protects heartbeat addresses with random tokens and stores webhook addresses and API keys encrypted;
4. engages Atlassian (Forge hosting) as the only sub-processor, which the Customer authorizes, and will
   inform the Customer of any intended change, giving it the opportunity to object;
5. assists the Customer, as far as possible, in responding to requests from data subjects and in meeting its
   obligations under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR;
6. notifies the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the
   App;
7. deletes the personal data at the end of the service as described in section 3;
8. makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and allows for audits in
   the form of written questionnaires answered within 30 days.

## 5. Services chosen by the Customer

When the Customer configures a stakeholder channel, the App sends alert data to the service the Customer
chose (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat, SendGrid or Resend), with the Customer's own credentials. These
services are selected and contracted by the Customer and are not sub-processors of the Provider; their
processing is governed by the Customer's agreements with them.

## 6. International transfers

Apart from the services in section 5, personal data stays in Atlassian's cloud. The Provider does not
transfer it elsewhere.
